This repository contains the data and analysis scripts associated with our comparative study of acoustic signal evolution in Hainan anurans.
We assembled morphological, ecological, vocalization and auditory data for 30 anuran species from Hainan, China. The analyses examine how morphology and calling environment are associated with interspecific variation in call characteristics and auditory sensitivity, and investigate the evolutionary relationship between vocalizations and hearing.

The dataset includes:
Morphological measurements
Ecological factors, including calling site and vegetation coverage
Call characteristics, including dominant frequency (DF), maximum frequency (Fmax) and note duration (ND)
Auditory traits derived from auditory brainstem response (ABR) measurements, including auditory threshold, best sensitivity frequency (BF) and high frequency hearing limit (HF)
Audio files after extracting single note for each species
Call spectra and ABR audiograms used for calculating spectral mismatch
Phylogenetic information
Main scripts used in our study
